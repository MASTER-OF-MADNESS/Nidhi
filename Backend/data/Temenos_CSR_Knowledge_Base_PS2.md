## Classification Rule: Direct CSR vs. Financial-Inclusion Enablement

For application logic, do **not** treat every Temenos social-impact or financial-inclusion relationship as a direct CSR grant.

Use these initiative types:

| Initiative Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `DIRECT_CSR` | Direct community investment / CSR activity |
| `COMMUNITY_INVESTMENT` | Community program or sponsorship |
| `EMPLOYEE_VOLUNTEERING` | Employee time/volunteering activity |
| `FINANCIAL_INCLUSION_ENABLEMENT` | Business/product enablement that creates social impact, not automatically a charitable grant |
| `ENVIRONMENTAL_OPERATION` | ESG/environmental operational activity |

Examples from this knowledge base:
- InstaBag SmartBin → `DIRECT_CSR`
- Threads for Change 2.0 → `DIRECT_CSR`
- CONIL Fest → `COMMUNITY_INVESTMENT`
- Romania tree planting → `EMPLOYEE_VOLUNTEERING` / `ENVIRONMENTAL_OPERATION`
- LOLC Cambodia → `FINANCIAL_INCLUSION_ENABLEMENT`
- CreditAccess Grameen → `FINANCIAL_INCLUSION_ENABLEMENT`

This classification prevents the AI from incorrectly claiming that every financial-inclusion client relationship represents direct CSR funding.

# PART 16 — COST EFFECTIVENESS

## Cost-Effectiveness Metrics

| Metric | Formula | Required Inputs | Advantages | Limitations | Primary/Secondary |
|--------|---------|-----------------|------------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Cost per Beneficiary** | Total Budget / Number of Direct Beneficiaries | Budget, beneficiary count | Simple, comparable across projects | Doesn't capture quality/depth of impact | PRIMARY |
| **Cost per Successful Outcome** | Total Budget / Number of Successful Outcomes | Budget, outcome count (e.g., employed trainees) | Measures effectiveness, not just reach | Requires clear outcome definition | PRIMARY |
| **Cost per Person Trained** | Total Budget / Number of People Trained | Budget, training completions | Useful for training projects | Doesn't measure skill gain or employment | SECONDARY (training-specific) |
| **Cost per Employment Generated** | Total Budget / Number of Employed Beneficiaries | Budget, employment count | Direct livelihood impact measure | Employment may not be sustained | PRIMARY (livelihood projects) |
| **Cost per Health Outcome** | Total Budget / Number of Health Improvements | Budget, health outcome count (e.g., lives saved) | Critical for health projects | Hard to attribute causality | SECONDARY (health-specific) |
| **Social Return on Investment (SROI)** | (Present Value of Impact / Total Investment) × 100 | Monetized impact value, investment | Comprehensive, monetizes social value | Complex, requires assumptions, not all impact monetizable | SECONDARY (advanced analysis) |
| **Impact per ₹1000** | (Impact Score × 1000) / Total Budget | Impact score, budget | Normalizes impact across budgets | Requires robust impact scoring | PRIMARY (for optimization) |
| **Beneficiary Reach Efficiency** | (Number of Beneficiaries / Budget) × 1,000,000 | Beneficiary count, budget | Measures scale efficiency | Doesn't capture impact quality | SECONDARY |

**Recommendation:**
- Use **Cost per Beneficiary** and **Cost per Successful Outcome** as PRIMARY metrics for initial screening
- Use **Impact per ₹1000** for optimization scoring
- Use **SROI** only for high-budget projects where detailed impact valuation is feasible
